Data is under ODbL 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Attribution is visible on the map. No online tile provider, API key, bulk tile download or offline tile cache is required. Route overlays are illustrative planning anchors, not GPS telemetry or a verified scheduled service. The original route diagram remains a manual/local fallback.
